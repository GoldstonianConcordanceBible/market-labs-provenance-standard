Purpose

Use this template for substantive DELL Market Lab artifacts, including:

* pre-event theses,
* live observations,
* post-event reviews,
* Control Tower records,
* model analyses,
* multi-model reviews,
* trade records,
* transcripts,
* experiment logs,
* and evidence reviews.

The objective is to preserve:

what was known, when it was known, who or what produced the analysis, what evidence supported it, and what happened afterward.

Do not rewrite an earlier artifact after the outcome becomes known.

Create a new linked artifact instead.
