Purpose

DELL Market Lab is an independent research and educational laboratory focused on the observation, documentation, and study of DELL as a reference asset.

The laboratory preserves what was known before an outcome, what occurred afterward, and how human and AI-assisted analytical systems interpreted the available evidence at each stage.

The objective is not to rewrite successful or unsuccessful market calls after the fact.

The objective is to preserve the research record.

⸻

Core Research Question

What did we know before the outcome?

Every substantive DELL Market Lab artifact should preserve enough provenance to distinguish:

* pre-event analysis,
* live observation,
* post-event review,
* verified market outcomes,
* author-originated interpretations,
* model-generated analysis,
* and later retrospective conclusions.

Provenance Standard

DELL Market Lab adopts the shared Market Labs Provenance Standard.

The standard defines:

* artifact schema,
* evidence classifications,
* naming conventions,
* disclosure requirements,
* and relationship rules.

Adoption of the standard does not merge DELL Market Lab with other laboratories using the same methodology.

⸻

Evidence Classes

The laboratory uses the following core evidence classifications:

* VERIFIED
* CORROBORATED
* AUTHOR-ORIGINATED
* PROPOSED
* FUTURE EXPERIMENT

Model agreement is not automatically treated as factual verification.

⸻

Research Stages

Market records may be classified as:

* PRE-EVENT
* LIVE
* POST-EVENT
* RETROSPECTIVE

A later outcome must not be used to silently rewrite an earlier thesis.

Where possible, the original pre-event artifact remains frozen and the later outcome is documented in a separate linked artifact.

⸻

AI-Assisted Analysis

DELL Market Lab may use multiple analytical systems, including human review and AI-assisted analysis.

Where applicable, records should distinguish:

* human-originated analysis,
* model-generated analysis,
* multi-model agreement,
* independently verified facts,
* and later outcomes.

Agreement among AI systems is analytical evidence, not independent factual corroboration.

⸻

Repository Principle

Public packaging may change. Provenance should not.

A YouTube title, thumbnail, Discord post, or social headline may change for clarity.

The underlying artifact ID, creation time, evidence status, and historical record should remain stable.
